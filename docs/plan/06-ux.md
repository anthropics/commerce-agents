# UX

Same components for every client. `theme.json` and `brand_voice` change the look and the wording.

## Screens

Launcher. A 48px round button in the bottom corner, in a shadow root, brand-colored, label “Ask”. No logo image.

First open. Panel title is the assistant name. Three starter chips from the client config: “A weekend camping kit”, “Help me compare”, “Check a size”. No pickup promise.

Product card. Image, name, brand, price, sale price when the record has one, “As of {date}”, rating and count, one reason clause, size chips when the family has options, and “Availability isn’t in this catalog.” The name opens the client PDP in a new tab.

Comparison. Two to four columns. Rows are the dimensions the shopper named. The price delta is the server’s. One column is marked recommended.

Filter chips. `present_suggestions`, at most four, each a short imperative.

Kit. Steps, each with a product and a line price. The total sits under the steps. Over budget is a label on the step, not a silent cheaper swap.

Cart. Lines, subtotal, and a “Checkout on {brand}” button. The copy says the order is not placed here.

Clarify. One question and chips that are the likely answers.

Empty. “Nothing matched”, plus a chip that broadens the search.

Error. The executor’s failure text, and a retry chip.

Escalation. Hours from config, and a link the client provides. Do not invent a phone number. The contact page fetched on 2026-10-15 lists `1-800-426-4840`. Leave it off the widget until the verify list is answered.

## Mobile

The panel is full screen. Cards stack. Comparison is a horizontal scroll with a sticky name column. Targets are at least 44px.

## Trust and accessibility

Every price is the tool record, with an as-of time. Sale price also says “Sale”, so color is not the only signal. Contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA on the token set. Focus rings are visible. Streamed text is a live region.

Voice rules, in addition to `brand_voice`: no medical or safety procedure (already in the shopping prompt), no membership benefit unless a policy tool returned it, and no “in stock” unless a fulfillment tool returned it.

## A second client

The same React components read CSS variables. A dark luxury theme replaces `--brand` and the display font fallback. No green from the first theme remains. The starter chips and the assistant name come from that client’s config, not from the REI copy.

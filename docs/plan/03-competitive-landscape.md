# Competitive landscape

Checked 2026-10-15. Each product below was confirmed from a public page that day. Checkout and ranking details in this market move quickly; re-check before a client pitch.

## Marketplace and search

Amazon Alexa for Shopping runs in the Amazon Shopping app, on the site, and on Echo Show. Amazon’s posts say it replaced the standalone Rufus chatbot, uses shopping history, compares products, shows up to a year of price history, and can auto-buy. Sources: [Alexa for Shopping](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-ai-assistant), [CNBC, 2026-05-13](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/amazon-ditches-rufus-ai-chatbot-in-favor-of-alexa-shopping-agent.html). A retailer does not own this agent. The price history and purchase authority are Amazon’s, on Amazon’s catalog.

Google AI Mode and Gemini, for signed-in adults in the US in English, can show a Buy button on eligible listings and check out with Google Pay. The merchant stays merchant of record. The protocol is the Universal Commerce Protocol. Sources: [Search Help](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16833721), [Merchant Center](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/16837055), [Gemini shopping](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16730149). The retailer does not own the conversation. The strength is distribution.

## General assistants

ChatGPT shopping is live in the US. OpenAI’s merchant page says the first Instant Checkout was dropped. Shoppers discover and compare in ChatGPT and buy on the merchant’s site or app. Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/), [chatgpt.com/merchants](https://chatgpt.com/merchants/), [CNBC, 2026-03-24](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/openai-revamps-shopping-experience-in-chatgpt-after-instant-checkout.html). That matches this plan’s checkout recommendation.

Microsoft Copilot Checkout launched in the US on Copilot.com on 2026-01-08, with PayPal, Shopify, and Stripe. The merchant stays merchant of record. Sources: [PayPal](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2026-01-08-PayPal-Powers-Microsofts-Launch-of-Copilot-Checkout), [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/01/08/microsoft-propels-retail-forward-with-agentic-ai-capabilities-that-power-intelligent-automation-for-every-retail-function/).

## One retailer’s assistant

Walmart Sparky, on Walmart’s own page, finds products and plans meals inside the Walmart experience. A March 2026 trade article says it also runs inside ChatGPT and that the cart syncs to Walmart. Sources: [walmart.com/cp/sparky](https://www.walmart.com/cp/sparky/5291783), [Envision Horizons](https://www.envisionhorizons.com/walmart-sparky-ai-product-discovery/). A directory at [ecommerceguide.com/agents](https://ecommerceguide.com/agents/) says checkout stays at the retailer. The page says its last full check was Sept 20 and does not print the year.

Perplexity shopping, as described by Shopify in 2026, syndicates product feeds and can buy through PayPal rather than through Shopify checkout. Source: [Shopify blog](https://www.shopify.com/blog/perplexity-shopping). Treat the checkout detail as second-hand.

## Vendor products

Shopify Sidekick is a merchant admin assistant, not a shopper agent. Agentic storefronts syndicate a catalog to ChatGPT, Google, and Copilot, with a switch for in-chat checkout versus a redirect to the store. Sources: [Shopify, Google](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/google), [Shopify, Microsoft](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/microsoft).

Salesforce Agentforce Commerce announced Shopper, Buyer, and Merchant agents as generally available on 2026-06-24, on the retailer’s commerce platform, with ChatGPT and Google connections described as arriving that summer. Source: [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentforce-commerce-announcement/). This is the closest vendor product to “the retailer owns the agent.”

Adobe Commerce describes a Commerce MCP server and Brand Concierge for a storefront assistant, and names UCP and ACP. Source: [Adobe](https://business.adobe.com/blog/adobe-commerce-summit-announcements).

## Outdoor retail

REI’s public site, fetched 2026-10-15, has editorial [Expert Advice](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice) and a [contact page](https://www.rei.com/about-rei/contact-us) that points product, order, and return questions to customer service or live chat. No public REI shopping agent was found. The [mobile app page](https://www.rei.com/mobile) says the app can check in-store availability and free same-day pickup when available. That capability is not in the feed.

Backcountry publishes AI search and merchandising case studies ([Netcore](https://netcore.ai/success-story/backcountry-ai-search-performance-case-study/), [Fermat](https://www.fermatcommerce.com/customers/how-backcountry-scaled-experimentation-across-their-product-ecosystem-with-fermat)) and describes a human Gearhead service ([SGB](https://sgbonline.com/backcountrys-kevin-lenau-discusses-ai-driven-transformation-outdoor-opportunity/)). Those pages are not a public agent specification.

## Where this blueprint is behind, and where it can win

The blueprint has no real catalog index, no embed, no as-of price, no merchant query analytics, no image search, and no voice.

A retailer-controlled agent can win on first-party catalog accuracy, brand voice, policy grounding, merchandising control, and, later, membership data the outside agents do not have.

It cannot win other retailers’ catalogs, stored payment credentials, a year of price history, auto-buy, or default placement in Google, Amazon, or ChatGPT. Do not pitch those.
